Common document checklist

દસ્તાવેજોની જરૂરિયાત ગ્રાહક, નોકરી/વ્યવસાય અને lender પ્રમાણે બદલાઈ શકે છે.

Identity & Address

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar / accepted address proof
  • Photographs and contact details

Income Documents

  • Salary slips / business income proof
  • Bank statements
  • ITR / Form 16, if applicable

Property Documents

  • Property papers / agreement, if available
  • Builder or seller details
  • Additional papers requested by lender

Please do not send Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements or other sensitive documents through this public website. First verify the agent and lender's official process, then use a secure channel.
